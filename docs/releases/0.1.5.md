### Tax data sources
- **GST rate:** [ATO GST guidance](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/how-gst-works) (updated 22 Jun 2023).
- **Resident tax brackets:** [ATO resident income tax rates](https://www.ato.gov.au/rates/individual-income-tax-rates/) (updated 17 Jun 2025).
- **Medicare levy thresholds:** [ATO Medicare levy reduction for low income earners](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/medicare-and-private-health-insurance/medicare-levy/medicare-levy-reduction/medicare-levy-reduction-for-low-income-earners) (updated 29 Jul 2025) and [family income thresholds](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/medicare-and-private-health-insurance/medicare-levy/medicare-levy-reduction/medicare-levy-reduction-family-income) (updated 12 Jun 2025).
- **Failure to lodge penalty:** [ATO guidance](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/paying-the-ato/interest-and-penalties/penalties/failure-to-lodge-on-time-penalty) (updated 15 Jun 2025) plus [Crimes Act 1914 s 4AA](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1914A00012/latest/text) confirming $330 penalty units.
- **General interest charge:** [ATO GIC rates](https://www.ato.gov.au/Rates/General-interest-charge-%28GIC%29-rates/) (updated 11 Jun 2025) and [Calculate and report ATO interest](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/paying-the-ato/interest-and-penalties/interest-we-charge/calculate-and-report-ato-interest) (updated 25 Jun 2025) noting the 1 Jul 2025 deductibility change.
- **Division 7A benchmark:** [ATO Division 7A benchmark interest rate](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/division-7a-benchmark-interest-rate/) (updated 8 Jul 2025).
